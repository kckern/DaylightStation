# Fullscreen vitals layout

`player/overlays/FullscreenVitalsOverlay.jsx` renders heart-rate avatars above
a shared equipment grid containing RPM devices and active pressure mats. Heart
rate avatars remain circular; pressure mats retain their distinct square face
and session totals. Clicking the overlay, or using Enter/Space while focused,
switches between the bottom-right and bottom-left corners. The voice-memo
button uses the top-right corner in fullscreen so it cannot cover a vitals
tile; outside fullscreen it keeps its existing bottom-right position.

The overlay observes its actual parent with `ResizeObserver`, including parent
resizes that do not resize the browser window. It never measures its own
transformed bounds. Observation stops when the overlay hides or unmounts.

The layout helper evaluates one through four columns with 88-pixel cells and
8-pixel gaps. Heart-rate rows and equipment rows are counted separately, while
RPM and mat tiles share equipment rows. It selects the largest uniform scale,
capped at 1, with the narrower arrangement winning a tie. The rendered panel
stays within the smaller of 320 pixels or 30% of parent width, and within 55%
of parent height. Uniform scaling keeps avatar circles round. Each 76-pixel
heart-rate avatar has room for its outer ring inside its cell.

`FullscreenVitalsOverlay.measure.test.jsx` mounts the real component and styles
in Chromium with synthetic participants. It checks six-person mixed equipment
geometry, circular avatars, both anchors, parent-only resizing, crowded-panel
bounds, observer cleanup, and collision with the actual voice-memo button.
It includes `FitnessPlayer.scss` as well as the canonical overlay stylesheet;
the former no longer carries a competing copy of the vitals layout. The screenshot generated by that test is a
synthetic review artifact, not a capture of household participants.
