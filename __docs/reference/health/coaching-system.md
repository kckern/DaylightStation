# Health Coaching System

The coaching layer turns assessed health evidence into short factual messages.
Scheduled notifications use `CoachingOrchestrator`; the health-coach agent also
provides result-only assignments, a persisted dashboard and interactive chat.
These paths share calorie assessments, with different delivery lifecycles.

For the budget contract see [Health](README.md#the-budget-equation--one-server-side-home).
For broader context see [Health system architecture](health-system-architecture.md)
and [Data pipeline](data-pipeline.md).

## Evidence and budget meanings

`BudgetService` owns counted food rows, workout merging/deduplication, dates,
weight and goal resolution, and daily budget assembly. Coaching does not rebuild
those calculations from nutrition summaries or legacy calorie targets.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Logging floor | `range.floor`, compared with **food**, for logging completeness |
| Base plan | `range.top`, compared with **net** (`food − exercise`) |
| Estimated maintenance | Break-even/TDEE, also compared with net |
| Food plan | Base plan plus canonical exercise credit, capped at food break-even by the shared tier rules |
| Food break-even | Estimated maintenance plus the canonical day's exercise credit |

Exercise is credited once, in full, under the existing Health policy. A day with
1,704 cal food, 997 cal exercise and a 1,600 cal base plan has a 2,597 cal food
plan and 893 cal remaining. Its food caption uses `1704 / 1200–2597 cal` and
labels the exercise adjustment. The logging floor stays 1,200 in this example.

Above the floor establishes logging completeness, not surplus. Above plan but
below estimated maintenance is over plan and still below maintenance. Surplus
means above estimated maintenance. Equality is neither over plan nor surplus.
These are estimates against the configured budget, not measured energy balance.

Historical budgets use **current configured goals** (`goalBasis: 'current'`).
Changing a goal can change a historical assessment. Historical goal versions are
not recovered; observation templates disclose the current-goal basis. Existing
saved messages remain historical records and are not regenerated by this change.

## Completeness, lifecycle and provenance

`portable/contracts/health/budgetAssessment.mjs` composes `budgetLines` and
`zoneFor`. It takes explicit `date` and `today`; it has no clock, persistence,
model, UI or messaging dependency.

- A log is complete when food reaches the budget floor, or the user declares it
  done/fasted or marks a meal skipped. Missing budget evidence cannot establish
  an intake verdict through a legacy goal fallback.
- A day is finished when its date precedes local today or it has an explicit
  day-level done/fasting closure. A skipped meal can complete today's log
  without finishing the day. Midnight finishes a day without completing its log.
- A complete live day can describe its current plan/maintenance position **so
  far**. Live partial totals never support a low-intake or protein-failure claim.
- Incomplete logs retain known boundaries but have unknown intake verdicts.
  Missing budgets are unavailable, never zero exercise. Unknown maintenance
  leaves maintenance verdicts unknown even when plan facts are known.
- Reconstructed nutrition is estimated intake with unknown protein. The
  evidence reader keeps that provenance even after a closure and suppresses
  calorie-plan and protein verdicts. Unknown nutrition provenance also suppresses
  intake verdicts. Neither becomes measured intake by joining it to a budget row.

`CoachingEvidenceService` bulk-loads one historical `getBudgetRange`, the
requested day's `getBudget`, nutrition provenance and closures. History ends the
day before the requested date, oldest first, retaining every calendar date and
per-day error. Actual local today comes from the injected clock and timezone,
independently of a backdated request. Snapshots retain source availability,
provenance, lifecycle, user ID and coverage. The composition root shares this
reader between agent budget tools and lightweight coaching.

`budgetPeriod.mjs` includes only finished, trustworthy assessments in energy
averages and counts. Coaching additionally requires logged provenance. Every
energy metric uses that same eligible-day set; if an eligible row lacks a metric,
that aggregate is null. Gaps remain in the coverage denominator. Protein is known
only with a finite measured macro and non-reconstructed provenance; measured zero
is distinct from unknown. `evidenceAverage` reports `proteinDays` separately and
status blocks label that denominator when it differs from calorie coverage.

`/done`, `/fast` and `/reopen` accept today, `yesterday` or a non-future date in
the user's timezone. Closures live in `users/{userId}/day_closed.yml`; a legacy
`true` means done. Meal fasts under `meals` record intentional empty meals without
closing the day. Summary-only classification can use household
`logging_completeness.min_calories` as a display fallback when the budget is
unavailable; it cannot restore shared intake judgments or eligible averages.

## Supported observations and selection

The lightweight pipeline is:

```
BudgetService + nutrition provenance → CoachingEvidenceService
  → snapshots + eligible observations → observation ID or silence
  → deterministic status block and observation text → messaging → receipt history
```

`buildObservations` offers structured facts with an ID, kind, covered date/window,
evidence dates, priority and fingerprint. Supported kinds are `logging_incomplete`,
`within_plan`, `over_plan`, `energy_surplus`, `protein_gap`, `activity_burn`,
`exercise_budget` and `period_summary`. Protein gaps require a finished,
trustworthy day, known protein and a finite positive target supplied to that
call. Period verdicts require trustworthy finished logs for at least half of the
calendar window; sparse periods can still report logging coverage. Daily
prominent foods are labelled as logged today; only explicit `eventItems` support
“just logged” wording. Templates do not advise eating the remaining calories.

`CoachingCommentaryService.generate(snapshot)` returns an offered Observation or
`null`. The model sees candidates under `current_evidence` and past messages
under `historical_context`. It must return exactly
`{"observationId":"an offered ID"}` or `{"observationId":null}`. Valid null means
silence. Malformed/extra output, unsupported IDs and provider failures use the
highest-priority unseen eligible observation, or silence if none remain.
Returned model prose is discarded. The runtime uses one step, no supplied tools
and a 30-second limit; its dedicated composition has no configured memory.

`renderObservation` produces plain text from the selected facts. Channel
formatting escapes that text once. Status blocks also format evidence directly;
the model cannot rewrite them. Morning reads yesterday plus seven historical
days; weekly loads 56 historical days and derives its seven-day window from
those same rows. Status blocks can show descriptive averages with partial
coverage even when coverage is insufficient for a period verdict.

Pattern detection supplies context, not additional model claims:

| Pattern | Rule |
|---|---|
| `missed_logging` | Latest historical day is incomplete or unlogged |
| `energy_surplus` | At least two of the last three eligible days exceed maintenance |
| `over_plan` | At least two of the last three eligible days exceed their own plan |
| `protein_short` | Known protein below 80% of target on at least three of the last five eligible days |
| `on_track` | Latest three calendar days are finished and trustworthy, within plan and at/above protein target |

Fingerprint metadata in delivered history suppresses recently delivered
observations. `observationFingerprints` also remembers bundled facts, including
a protein gap inside a within-plan sentence. A newly finished protein gap stays
eligible after an earlier live within-plan message. Legacy history without this
metadata remains readable; it cannot provide the same factual repetition guard.
History load/save failures can also weaken repetition prevention.

## Agent assignments and dashboard

Legacy `MorningBrief`, `WeeklyDigest`, `EndOfDayReport` and `ExerciseReaction`
return validated payloads to their callers. `BaseAgent` does not deliver those
results. Their validation replaces unrestricted model nutrition prose with
shared observations; malformed/schema-invalid model output gets deterministic
fallback, while valid `should_send: false` preserves silence. Existing configured
compliance/pattern CTAs remain. Morning streaks require contiguous eligible
calendar dates; weekly comparisons match each day to its own adjusted plan.

Programmatic gathering retains its tools and existing reconciliation workflows.
Automated assignment reasoning receives **`tools: []` and per-request
`memory: false`**. The runtime adapter omits both configured SDK Memory and its
execution scope for that request, preventing SDK-added memory mutation tools.
Interactive chat retains its existing tools and memory behavior.

`DailyDashboard` retains its schema and curated content selection. Coach briefing
uses canonical observations; mixed model coach CTAs/prompts are discarded. A
fitness CTA is rendered only when the selected content ID resolves to gathered
canonical content, using that title. `HealthCoachAgent` explicitly persists the
dashboard after validated execution returns. This is separate from notification
receipt history. The deterministic guarantee covers automated coach/nutrition
assessment prose; arbitrary curated fitness rationale and conversational chat
are outside that guarantee. Invalid dashboard schemas still fail validation.

## Triggers, delivery and failure limits

The current composition selects one coached user (head of household) and one
configured conversation. Reads and receipts are scoped by user; it does not
schedule a separate notification for every household member.

| Trigger | Implemented behavior |
|---|---|
| Post-meal/post-report | A committed capture, Undo or generated report re-arms a quiet timer (default ten minutes). Only today's changes for the coached user arm it. A timer firing in configured `post_meal.quiet_hours` (default 22:00–07:00) is dropped, not queued. Empty commentary skips the notification. |
| Morning brief | Scheduler calls the lightweight orchestrator using `morning_brief.schedule` (default `0 10 * * *`). |
| Weekly digest | Scheduler calls the lightweight orchestrator using `weekly_digest.schedule` (default `0 19 * * 0`). |
| Exercise reaction | Enrichment evaluates the fetched activity: started today locally and burn above `exercise_reaction.min_calories` (default 200). Unknown/trivial burn does not start automated assignment reasoning. |
| Legacy assignments/on-demand/dashboard | Callers receive validated results; dashboard persistence is explicit. These definitions do not establish an additional scheduled delivery path. |

Activity burn is separate from canonical daily exercise credit. Until its ID
appears in the canonical sessions, text labels activity synchronization pending
and assumes no extra credit. It never adds webhook burn to an already adjusted
budget.

The orchestrator sends through the messaging gateway, then records history.
Exercise reactions deduplicate by activity ID in that user's saved history.
Post-report sends save the new message receipt and then attempt to delete the
previous post-report message. Deletion failure is logged and can leave both
messages visible. Timer coalescing and recorded receipts do not establish
exactly-once transport delivery or a general daily/weekly orchestrator dedupe.

Send failures are logged; no successful receipt is recorded, so a later eligible
invocation can try again. There is no orchestrator backoff queue or guaranteed
dashboard fallback for failed sends. Persistence failure after a successful send
is logged and can leave repetition/idempotency history incomplete. No universal
quiet-hours hold/release queue is implemented for morning, weekly or exercise
messages; the documented quiet-hour drop applies to the meal trigger.

Missing budget/nutrition sources retain explicit unavailable/estimated states.
They never trigger a fallback surplus judgment from legacy goals. Model failures
cannot introduce contrary calorie verdicts because delivery renders supported
facts. These safeguards do not guarantee source accuracy, recovered historical
goals, transport delivery, or medical interpretation.

## Where it lives

Paths below are relative to the repository root.

- `__app_modules/wellness/health/portable/contracts/health/` — budget zones, tiers,
  daily assessment and period aggregation; browser-safe pure contracts.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/server/application/health/BudgetService.mjs` —
  canonical budget assembly.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/server/application/coaching/` — evidence reader,
  patterns, snapshots, observations, templates, status blocks, selector,
  orchestrator and `MealCoachingTrigger.mjs`.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/server/composition/coaching.mjs` — shared evidence
  wiring, lightweight scheduler registration and meal trigger.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/server/application/agents/health-coach/` — agent,
  assignments and budget tools.
- `__capabilities/agent-runtime/server/application/framework/` — assignment and
  BaseAgent lifecycle; `server/adapters/MastraAdapter.mjs` under the same capability
  honors request-scoped memory opt-out.
- `__app_modules/wellness/fitness/server/application/webhookCoachingPolicy.mjs` —
  fetched-activity eligibility and reaction projection.
- `__app_modules/wellness/nutribot/server/application/usecases/reportCaption.mjs` —
  exercise-consistent report captions.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/server/adapters/persistence/yaml/YamlHealthDatastore.mjs`
  — per-user `health_coaching.yml`, nutrition and closure persistence.
- `__app_modules/wellness/health/browser/today/budgetStory.js` — shared assessment
  flags with existing Health presentation; `browser/CoachChat/` under the same
  module is interactive chat.

Household coaching configuration is `household/coaching/config.yml`; user goals
are `users/{userId}/apps/health/goals.yml`. No schedule change, goal recalibration,
history rewrite or production migration is part of the shared-evidence change.
