# Household Agenda

Agenda organizes a recurring series into independent weekly plans. A series holds a weekly calendar and defaults; a plan has its own date, people, ordered activities, and draft or publication state. Copying a plan or creating one from defaults gives the new week its own identities and settings. Editing one week does not edit another.

Administrators use **Content → Agendas** to view this week and next week, edit a plan and its roster, and publish it. The plan editor stages changes until Save or Publish. Publish saves the draft first and checks activity setup. A revision conflict retains the local buffer for reload or reapplication. Named guests come from the shared people directory; anonymous guests are distinct plan-local entries. Named people are shown with their household label and portrait from the people directory ("Dad", "Mom"); only anonymous guests keep a plan-local name.

Participant-aware activities can use everyone on the plan or an explicit ordered subset. The activity's registered editor owns its settings, such as Charades clue modes. Removing someone from a subset clears that person's associated settings. Only explicitly casual Charades definitions opt in through the Party Games consumer; competitive Charades and other mounted games retain their normal behavior without a binding. Existing participant bindings on ineligible targets fail validation.

A TV menu opens the published current plan or a dated plan. Screens never sign in: a TV is identified by its declared device, and only the Admin editor requires sign-in. Past or future plans are previews; missing, draft, cancelled, or archived plans do not silently run another week. Starting a participant-aware activity goes straight in: an engaged game in progress resumes, otherwise a new one starts. A review screen appears only when the activity's setup asks for confirmation. Returning from an activity refreshes the dated plan and restores the selected activity. An existing session retains its original participants and setup after later plan changes.

Canonical Agenda enablement and transfer of an existing legacy list are separate operations.
