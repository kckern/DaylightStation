## Required corrections

1. **Lesson02, `seems-02`, formB: two plausible answers.** Prompt: `Infer a current quality: 이 음식이 ___. (delicious)`. Key: `맛있는 것 같아요`; distractor: `맛있을 것 같아요`. A speaker looking at untasted food can naturally use `이 음식이 맛있을 것 같아요` to infer that the food is delicious. The English current-quality instruction does not rule out that ordinary conjectural use of -(으)ㄹ 것 같다. Smallest correction: replace the distractor `맛있을 것 같아요` with `맛있다고 해요`, which reports hearsay rather than inferring. Alternatively explicitly require the present 있다/없다 attributive form in the prompt, although that reduces meaningful application. The workbook's p13 restaurant-food inference exercise demonstrates why an untasted-food judgment is a natural context here.

2. **Lessons04 and06, model `late-fear`: inaccurate RR.** `지각할까 봐 뛰어왔어.` currently has `jigakhalkka bwa ttwieowasseo`. Korean ㄱ followed by ㅎ is aspirated ㅋ, represented by `k` in Revised Romanization, not the sequence `kh`. Smallest correction in both reused model entries: `jigakalkka bwa ttwieowasseo`. Source Korean and page48 are correct.

3. **Lessons05 and06, model `vacation`: inaccurate month reading.** `6월 23일부터 방학이래요.` currently has `yugwol isipsamilbuteo banghagiraeyo`. June is read 유월, not 육월. Smallest correction in both reused model entries: `yuwol isipsamilbuteo banghagiraeyo`. The Korean numeric spelling and source p58 are correct.
