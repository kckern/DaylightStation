## Required corrections

1. **L15 `review-thing-02`, Form A:** `자막 없이 ___ 힘들어요.` keyed `보는 것이`, with distractor `보느라고`. The full distractor sentence `자막 없이 보느라고 힘들어요` is natural Korean: the speaker is having difficulty because they are watching without subtitles. The stated intent “Watching without subtitles is difficult” does not adequately distinguish it from the nominalized subject sentence. **Suggested correction:** begin the intent “Make watching the subject: Watching without subtitles is difficult…” (or otherwise require the -는 것 subject construction), or replace 보느라고 with an unambiguously invalid subject form.

2. **L15 `review-thing-02`, Form B:** `팔을 다쳐서 글을 ___ 힘들어요.` keyed `쓰는 것이`, with distractor `쓰느라고`. `팔을 다쳐서 글을 쓰느라고 힘들어요` is natural: with the injured arm, writing is tiring/difficult. It can meet the current English intent “Writing with an injured arm is difficult.” **Suggested correction:** explicitly require making the activity the subject, or replace 쓰느라고. Both forms of this target need the same repair.

3. **L16 all twenty question feedback strings:** each repeats the entire corresponding pattern explanation verbatim. This fails the requested focused-feedback standard and misses the important specific learning error. **Suggested correction:** replace each question explanation with one or two targeted sentences addressing the actual A/B contrast. Examples: `plain-style-01` should explain completed yesterday → 읽었다 versus present now → 읽는다; `reported-noun-01` should explain vowel-final 가수래요 versus consonant-final 회사원이래요; `background-01` should explain adjective 아픈데 versus action verb 먹는데; `reported-question-01` should explain verb 먹느냬요 versus irregular adjective 덥다 → 더우냬요. Apply comparable question-specific feedback to the other sixteen final targets. L13–15 feedback is already focused.
